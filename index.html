THE MAIN IDEA

Our idea

Give Hope, Not Pity. Four things guide everything LDF does.

Every person has value

Whatever your religion, politics or means, you have something to give.

Help with dignity, proved

Families are chosen fairly: most need first, then longest waiting. Every package is proved with photos.

Learn and grow together

Courses for real life, free tutoring time in every plan, and Coaches approved by LDF beside you.

Free where it matters

Sponsors pay for free places. Premium and paid courses by our Coaches keep LDF running.

FOR COMPANIES AND SPONSORS

Partner with LDF

Companies, banks and local businesses can sponsor real, measurable good. Every rupee is tracked openly in the LDF app, and you get honest reports, not exaggerated stories.

01

Sponsor free tutoring

Pay for the free tutoring time learners get with LDF-approved Coaches, so no one is left behind because they cannot pay.

02

Sponsor Hope packages

Fund food packages for families in a village or district. Families are chosen fairly and every delivery is proved with photos.

03

Sponsor courses

Help us make courses free in Sinhala, Tamil and English on careers, life skills and wellbeing.
