THE MAIN IDEA

Our idea

Give Hope, Not Pity. Four things guide everything LDF does.

Every person has value

Whatever your religion, politics or means, you have something to give.

Help with dignity, proved

Families are chosen fairly: most need first, then longest waiting. Every package is proved with photos.

Learn and grow together

Courses for real life, free tutoring time in every plan, and Coaches approved by LDF beside you.

Free where it matters

LDF courses are free, taught by volunteer Coaches. Sponsors, partners and a small fee on Hope deliveries keep LDF running.

WHY WE DO IT THIS WAY

Nobody should have to choose between dinner and learning.

Prices keep rising in Sri Lanka. Fuel, food, bus fares and school things all cost more than they did. Many families are doing everything right and still falling behind. LDF is built so that help reaches them without a bill attached.

Learning stays free

A course should never cost a family a meal. So LDF’s courses are free, in Sinhala, Tamil and English. Our Coaches and counsellors give their time as volunteers, so a child in a village gets the same chance as a child in a city.

Everyone gives what they have

Volunteers give their day. Shops give a discount. Companies sponsor a programme day. Donors give food. Nobody gives everything, but together it is enough, and nobody who receives help is made to feel small.

Fair to the people who help

The district teams who travel to villages are paid fairly for that day’s work, and their travel and meals are covered. Day sponsors make this possible. Every rupee is shown openly in the app.

This is what Give Hope, Not Pity means to us: help that respects people, costs them nothing, and is shared by a whole community.

PATHFINDER · FREE IN THE APP

Stuck with a problem? Find your way forward.

Exams went wrong, a hard decision at home, not sure what to do next? Tell Pathfinder in your own words, in English, Sinhala or Tamil. It asks a few questions, explores around 30 possible paths from every angle, and shows the ones that fit you best, with first steps for this week.

  • Private: what you write is not saved
  • Talk any path through: “What if my parents get angry?”
  • Want a real person? Pathfinder can link you to a counsellor checked by LDF
Try Pathfinder Talk to a counsellor

Pathfinder is a computer helper, not a counsellor, doctor or lawyer. If you are in danger, call 1926 (24 hours) or 119 now.

How it works

  1. Describe the problem in a few sentences. No names or phone numbers needed.
  2. Answer a few questions so Pathfinder understands your situation.
  3. See your best paths, each scored for how well it fits you, with first steps.
