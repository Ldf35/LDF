NOVA - your AI team for Life Development Foundation
====================================================
Nova runs on THIS laptop. Keep this folder private: never upload it to GitHub,
Vercel or anywhere public (config.json holds your keys).

ONE-TIME SETUP
1. Install Python 3.12 or 3.13 from python.org  (tick "Add python.exe to PATH")
   and Ollama from ollama.com  (you have both already).
2. Double-click  "1 - SETUP (run once).bat"  and wait until it says Done.
3. Open  config.json  in Notepad and fill in:
     your_name          your name
     supabase_key       Supabase -> Project Settings -> API -> service_role key
     your_ldf_user_id   Supabase -> Authentication -> Users -> your row -> User UID
     website_url        your website address (e.g. https://ldf.lk)
   Optional: github_token, vercel_token, vercel_project, vercel_deploy_hook,
             owner_pin (4 digits), phone_access true + phone_pin (6+ digits).
   Already filled in for you: LDF details, Supabase address, email via your app,
   logo, currency LKR.

LDF'S NATURAL VOICE AND FEELINGS
  LDF now speaks with a natural human-like voice (it downloads once, about 60 MB, the
  first time it talks). The mouth moves with the real voice, on the laptop and the phone.
  LDF also picks its own mood and move for each answer (happy, concerned, thinking...
  wave, thumbs up, celebrate...). Sinhala and Tamil replies still use the Windows voice.
  If the voice sounds wrong:  add  "voice_engine": "windows"  to config.json.
  Another voice:  "piper_voice": "en_US-amy-medium"  (any name from rhasspy/piper-voices).

LDF'S SIZE: right-click LDF on your screen -> LDF's size -> Small / Medium / Big / Huge.
  (or put  "pet_size": 150  in config.json; 100 is normal)

FINANCE AGENT (read-only: it never pays or moves money)
  "How is our money?"  "What does LDF owe?"  "Check the money for problems"
  Dashboard -> Finance: money in, LDF's own share, money out, what LDF owes, gifts to match, batch money.

LDF IN 3D starts by itself. It shows your agents at work: glowing task orbs when the
Brain hands out a job, the agent's result in a bubble, and what's next for you.
Click it to ask, drag it, right-click for more. Keep these next to my_agent.py:
the "ldf_frames" folder, three.min.js and ldf3d.js (they make LDF 3D).
To turn that off: "mini_nova_on_start": false in config.json.

Full instructions for every agent are at the top of my_agent.py.
