LIFE DEVELOPMENT FOUNDATION

Who we are

Life Development Foundation exists to encourage personal growth and positive community development through knowledge, practical skills and responsible action.

Our vision

Creating a positive and thriving community involves nurturing spirituality and fostering good attitudes, alongside the acquisition of knowledge and skills.

Our mission

Spreading knowledge and organising workshops to inspire societal change, helping people find mental happiness, and fostering responsible citizens who contribute to their country.

Our approach

We believe sustainable progress grows when people are treated with dignity, have access to useful knowledge and can take part in solutions that strengthen their communities.

Organisational transparency

Publish registration details, leadership names, policies and official documents here only after they have been verified and approved for public release.

Our values

  • Dignity and respect
  • Learning and personal responsibility
  • Transparency and accountability
  • Collaboration and community participation
  • Privacy, safety and fairness
