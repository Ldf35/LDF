LIFE DEVELOPMENT FOUNDATION

Who we are

Life Development Foundation (LDF) is a community-development initiative in Sri Lanka, built on genuine humanity, positive attitudes and shared growth.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

Every person has a genuine side.

Whatever their religion, politics, income or past, every human being has something good to give. When people are treated with respect and given the chance to learn, they grow, and they help others grow.

That is why our motto is Give Hope, Not Pity. We help without making anyone feel small, and we invite everyone to contribute, not only to receive.

Our mission

To spread knowledge and organise workshops that inspire societal change, support mental happiness, and grow responsible citizens who contribute positively to their communities and country.

Our vision

A positive, thriving community that nurtures spirituality (reflection, compassion and responsibility, with no particular religion required), good attitudes, knowledge and skills.

WHAT WE DO

Five areas of work

Child protection

Safer, more supportive places where children can learn and grow. Safeguarding comes first, always.

Family empowerment

Workshops and practical support that help families become stronger and more independent.

Career development

CV and interview help, employability skills and mentoring. We give honest guidance, never guaranteed jobs.

Academic success

Free courses, study skills and volunteer tutors, so learning is open to everyone.

Mental health and wellbeing

Awareness sessions on resilience, stress and healthy relationships, and links to qualified professionals.

In the app

Give to families through LDF Hope, learn with the Learning Hub, get help from volunteer tutors, and join as a volunteer, partner or shop.

OUR VALUES

How we work

  • Humanity: every person has dignity
  • Honesty and transparency
  • Equality: every religion, background and belief is welcome
  • Knowledge shared, not kept
  • Responsibility and cooperation
  • Help without pity

What LDF is not

  • Not a political party. We do not campaign for anyone.
  • Not promoting one religion over another.
  • Not a substitute for medical, legal or financial advice.
  • Never publishing private details of families or children.
