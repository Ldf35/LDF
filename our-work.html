OUR APPROACH

Building stronger lives and thriving communities

LDF's work connects learning, practical support, personal development and community participation. We aim to treat every person with equal respect, protect individual dignity and encourage people to build confidence and independence.

The programmes described on this page represent our focus areas. Specific services, activities and referral options depend on the arrangements, resources and approvals available for each programme.
