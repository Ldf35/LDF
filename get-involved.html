A CLEAR PROCESS

What Happens Next?

Every enquiry should be handled fairly, transparently and according to the needs of the relevant programme.

01

Tell Us About Yourself

Choose an opportunity and share your interests, relevant skills and preferred way to contribute.

02

Review and Discuss

LDF can review your enquiry and discuss relevant requirements, availability and next steps.

03

Agree on Next Steps

If a suitable opportunity is available, the relevant arrangements, responsibilities and safeguards can be agreed.
