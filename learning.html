HOW TO START

Three easy steps

  1. Create a free account in the LDF app.
  2. Choose a course and start the first lesson.
  3. Ask for help from the AI Coach or an LDF-approved Coach.

For teachers and professionals

Share what you know. Coaches are approved by LDF and give some of their time to tutor learners. They can also run their own paid courses and keep 70% of what they earn; the rest helps keep LDF’s own courses free for everyone.

WHY IT IS FREE

Because someone once taught us too

Prices keep rising in Sri Lanka, and it is often learning that a family gives up first. We do not want anyone to stop learning because of money. Our Coaches give their time, sponsors and partners cover the costs, and every learner can start today without paying a rupee.
