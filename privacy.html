Respecting privacy and personal information

We believe that trust begins with respect. Protecting personal information, handling enquiries responsibly and safeguarding the privacy of individuals are important considerations in LDF's work.

Important: review required before publication

This page is a policy framework, not legal advice or a confirmed description of LDF's data practices. Before publishing it as a final policy, LDF must verify its activities, applicable legal obligations, data handling arrangements, service providers, contact details and retention periods.

This policy should be updated whenever LDF introduces a new website form, application, analytics service, online learning feature or other activity that changes how personal information is collected or used.

1. Information we may collect

The final policy must identify the personal information actually collected through the LDF website, contact forms, applications, learning services and other connected systems.

Depending on the services in use, this could include:

  • Contact details and information submitted through enquiries.
  • Information provided when registering for a programme.
  • Learning, volunteer or partnership information where applicable.
  • Technical information processed by website hosting or connected service providers.

These are examples only. LDF should list only information it actually collects or receives and should explain whether providing each type of information is optional or necessary.

2. How information is used

LDF must explain the specific purposes for which personal information is processed and identify the applicable lawful basis where required.

Depending on actual operations, purposes may include:

  • Responding to enquiries and requests.
  • Coordinating approved programmes and activities.
  • Managing authorised volunteer or partner participation.
  • Maintaining appropriate records and meeting legal obligations.
  • Protecting the security and proper operation of services.

Information should not be used for unrelated purposes without an appropriate justification and any required notice or consent.

3. Sharing and storage

The final policy must identify relevant hosting providers, application platforms, communication tools and other organisations that may process information for LDF.

It should explain:

  • Who may access personal information and why.
  • How information is protected against unauthorised access.
  • Whether information is transferred to other countries.
  • How long different categories of information are retained.
  • When and how information is securely deleted.

Information should be shared only where there is an appropriate reason and suitable safeguards. LDF should not promise complete security or claim that information is never shared unless those statements can be substantiated.

4. Your choices and rights

Depending on the applicable law and circumstances, individuals may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection to the processing of their personal information, as well as other rights.

The final policy should explain how to submit a request, what information is needed to handle it safely, how LDF will respond and how a person may raise a concern with the appropriate supervisory authority where applicable.

5. Children and sensitive information

LDF recognises the importance of protecting children, families and people who may be in vulnerable circumstances. Personal information relating to these individuals requires particular care.

  • Collect only information that is necessary for a defined and appropriate purpose.
  • Apply suitable safeguarding, access controls and confidentiality procedures.
  • Avoid requesting sensitive personal details through general public contact forms.
  • Do not publish identifiable family details, private addresses or children's photographs without appropriate authority, lawful justification and safeguards.

Any programme involving children or sensitive information should have suitable procedures in place before information is collected.

6. Cookies and third-party services

LDF must confirm whether its website or connected services use cookies, analytics, embedded content or other technologies that collect information from visitors.

Where applicable, the policy should explain what these technologies do, which providers are involved and what choices visitors have. Any consent mechanism required by applicable law must be implemented separately where necessary.

7. Changes to this policy

The final policy should explain how material changes will be communicated and identify when the policy was last reviewed or updated.
