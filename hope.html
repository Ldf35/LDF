ONE BATCH, MANY HANDS

Who finishes a Hope batch?

Donors give the food

Anyone can give towards the food packages for one village. Every rupee shows in the batch’s public money breakdown.

Sponsors cover the day

Up to 3 companies or people cover the programme day: travel to the village, meals and fair pay for the district team.

Shops and volunteers deliver

A local LDF Hope partner shop packs the parcels, and the district team delivers them with photo proof.

When the last rupee comes in, everyone who helped hears the same words: “We did it together.” Then everyone gets the completion report with photos.

HOW IT WORKS

From your gift to a family’s table

  1. A family is identified. LDF works through a documented, fair process with trusted community representatives. Families are never shamed or made public.
  2. You choose a village or batch. In the app you see which village is being helped, how much food is still needed and whether the programme day still needs a sponsor.
  3. The community finishes it. When donors have covered the food and sponsors have covered the day, the batch goes ahead.
  4. Local shops pack the parcels. Approved local shops prepare the packages, so the money stays in the community.
  5. We prove it with photos. Packing and delivery photos are checked by two LDF team members before you see them.
  6. Everyone sees the result. Donors and sponsors receive the completion report with checked photos. Families’ names and addresses are never shown.

FOR COMPANIES AND PEOPLE

Sponsor a programme day

Every Hope delivery is a day of work for a local team. Costs have gone up across Sri Lanka, so we ask sponsors to cover the day itself, while donors pay for the food.

  1. Choose a day. In the app, pick a village batch and tap “Sponsor this programme day”.
  2. Transfer the money. You get a reference and LDF’s bank details. Up to 3 sponsors can share one day.
  3. LDF confirms it. Our staff check the bank statement and thank you. Your name appears on the batch and, if you agree, on our supporters page.
  4. See the result. After the delivery is checked, you receive the report with photos, and every rupee shows in the batch’s public money breakdown.

What Rs 25,000 covers

An example day: about 20 families, a district leader, a team lead and 3 volunteers.

  • Transport to the village and back (about 60 km): Rs 7,200
  • Phones and small costs: Rs 1,000
  • Meals and water for the team: Rs 3,000
  • Fair pay for the team: about Rs 13,000

Day costs are paid first; everything else goes to the team. Amounts are estimates from October 2026 prices and vary by village. You can give from Rs 5,000.
