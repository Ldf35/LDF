Hope for Families

LDF Hope turns kindness into food packages for families who need a hand, with dignity, proof and honest updates.

Live from the LDF app. Only deliveries LDF has checked and approved are counted.

HOW IT WORKS

From your gift to a family’s table

  1. A family is identified. LDF works through a documented, fair process with trusted community representatives. Families are never shamed or made public.
  2. You choose a village or batch. In the app you see which village is being helped and how many food packages are needed.
  3. Local shops pack the parcels. Approved local shops prepare the packages, so the money stays in the community.
  4. We prove it with photos. Packing and delivery photos are checked by two LDF team members before you see them.
  5. You see the result. Donors see verified updates. Families’ names and addresses are never shown.

Our promises

  • Help with dignity, never pity
  • Clear eligibility, free from favouritism
  • Proof photos for every delivery
  • Honest updates, no exaggerated stories
  • No pressure, guilt or fear to give
  • We never promise help that is not funded
