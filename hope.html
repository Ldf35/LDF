OUR APPROACH

Support That Respects Every Family

HOPE is designed to offer practical grocery support while protecting the dignity, privacy and equal worth of the people involved. The aim is to build a responsible process that donors, local partners, shops and volunteers can understand.

Project activities depend on available resources, appropriate approvals and arrangements confirmed by LDF. Public information will be updated as verified progress becomes available.

OUR PRINCIPLES

Hope With Responsibility

Every part of the project should be guided by dignity, fairness, privacy and accountability.

01

Dignity First

Every family deserves respect. Support should never depend on public exposure, embarrassment or sharing personal hardship for publicity.

02

Responsible Selection

Family referrals and eligibility decisions should follow appropriate verification procedures and documented priorities.

03

Transparent Records

Contributions, grocery packages, delivery evidence and payments should be recorded and reviewed through appropriate controls.

THE PROCESS

How the Process Is Designed to Work

HOPE follows a planned process intended to help support reach eligible families responsibly. The steps below describe the intended workflow, not a claim that every stage is currently operational.

  1. Identify Needs Responsibly

    Families may be considered through appropriate local referrals and verification procedures, subject to safeguarding, confidentiality and applicable requirements.

  2. Plan Support Fairly

    Eligible support is organised into batches according to documented priorities, available resources and approved project arrangements.

  3. Coordinate Shops and Volunteers

    Participating shops can prepare agreed grocery packages, while approved volunteers help coordinate project activities and distribution.

  4. Review Delivery Evidence

    Appropriate records are reviewed to help confirm that planned support was delivered, while limiting access to sensitive family information.

  5. Review Payments and Records

    Relevant package costs, supporting evidence and payment records should be checked and approved by authorised personnel before payments are processed.

  6. Report Accurately

    Project updates, contribution records and financial summaries are published only when the information has been checked and approved for release.

Our Privacy Commitment

Family names, home addresses, identifiable photographs and personal circumstances must not be published on this public website without an appropriate lawful basis, informed permission where required, and suitable safeguards.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the HOPE Project?

HOPE is LDF's family-support initiative, designed around grocery assistance, responsible coordination, privacy and accountability.

How are families considered?

The intended approach is to use appropriate referrals, verification and documented priorities. Contact LDF for information about the current arrangements.

Can I donate directly through this page?

This page does not collect payments. Contact LDF to confirm whether an approved contribution method is currently available. Always verify payment details before sending money.

Can shops or volunteers participate?

Interested shops and volunteers can contact LDF to discuss suitable roles, requirements and approval procedures.

Will family details be published?

Sensitive family information should be kept confidential. Public reporting should use appropriately checked information and suitable privacy safeguards.
