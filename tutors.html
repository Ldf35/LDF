Coaches and tutoring

One-to-one help from Coaches approved by LDF, online and safe. Every plan includes free tutoring time.

FOR LEARNERS

Stuck on something? Ask a Coach.

Every plan includes free tutoring time with a Coach approved by LDF, on video calls (Zoom, Google Meet or Microsoft Teams). Each course also has an AI Coach for quick questions.

  • Free tutoring time in every plan: 30 minutes, or 60 with Premium
  • Sessions are recorded for safety
  • Under-18s need a guardian’s consent and a guardian present
  • Coaches never take money or private contact details from learners in tutoring sessions
  • Free tutoring time is paid for by app plans and sponsors

Become a Coach

Are you a teacher, student, professional or just good at explaining? Join as a Coach: accept our safeguarding agreement, get approved by LDF, and give some tutoring time to learners. You can also run your own paid courses and keep 70% of every sale; 30% helps LDF run.

All Coaches are adults and are approved by LDF before they appear in the app.
