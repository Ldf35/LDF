FOR LEARNERS

Stuck on something? Ask a Coach.

Volunteer Coaches give free 30-minute online sessions on video calls (Zoom, Google Meet or Microsoft Teams). Each course also has an AI Coach for quick questions.

  • Free 30-minute sessions (60 for Premium members)
  • Sessions are recorded for safety
  • Under-18s need a guardian’s consent and a guardian present
  • Tutors are volunteers; they never take money or private contact details
