OUR COMMITMENT

Accountability in practice

Impact reporting should explain what happened, what evidence supports the results, how resources were used and what can be improved. We aim to communicate clearly without exaggerating achievements or making unsupported claims.

Information is published only when it has been appropriately checked and approved for public release. Where information cannot be verified, it should not be presented as a confirmed result.

WHAT WE MEASURE

What we report

Programme activity

A clear description of activities, reporting periods, appropriate location details and whether work is planned, ongoing or completed.

Verified outcomes

Results supported by relevant records and reviewed before publication. Activity counts and outcomes are distinguished from longer-term impact.

Financial accountability

Approved summaries of funds received, project expenditure and relevant operating costs, with appropriate supporting records and explanations.

Learning and improvement

Lessons from project delivery, challenges identified, improvements made and next steps under consideration.

PEOPLE FIRST

Transparency with dignity

Accountability does not require exposing the private lives of the people an organisation supports. LDF aims to report responsibly and avoid publishing identifiable family details, private addresses, sensitive personal circumstances or photographs without an appropriate lawful basis and safeguards.

Reports should use aggregated or anonymised information where appropriate, especially when children and vulnerable people may be involved.
