HOW IT WORKS

Three ways to take part

Buy from local shops

  1. Choose an item in the app.
  2. Enter your phone and address. Only that shop sees them.
  3. The shop delivers, and you pay the shop when it arrives, by cash or bank transfer.

Give a free item

  1. Take 1 to 4 photos of something clean, safe and useful.
  2. Your district team checks it before it appears.
  3. A partner shop picks it up and takes it to someone who needs it. You get a thank-you when it arrives.

Receive a free item

  1. Ask for a free item near you.
  2. A partner shop tells you the small delivery fee first.
  3. You pay only that fee, to the shop, on delivery. The item itself is free.

FOR SHOPS

Sell with LDF

Bring your shop to LDF Market and reach families across your district. Joining is free, and you keep delivering the way you do now.

  • Free to join, and LDF takes no percentage from any sale
  • A free shop shows up to 10 items at a time
  • You deliver, and the buyer pays you directly
  • Earn from delivering free items in your district, and keep the whole fee
  • LDF checks every shop, and certified shops get a badge

Free account or Shop plan

  • Free account: up to 10 items showing, orders and free-item deliveries, no fees
  • Shop plan, Rs 5,000 a month: unlimited items, your items first in the lists, and a "Shop plan" badge
  • Pay the Shop plan by bank transfer; LDF confirms it in a day
  • Example: a Rs 2,500 sale. The shop keeps all Rs 2,500 and the delivery fee

New shops: register in the app first. LDF checks every shop before it can sell.

Safe and checked

  • Every free item is checked by the district team before it is listed
  • No medicine, weapons, alcohol, tobacco or broken electricals
  • Phone numbers and addresses are shared only with the shop handling the order
  • Anyone can report a problem on an order, and LDF follows up
  • Shops that break the rules or do not pay are paused

WHY WE DO THIS

Nothing useful should go to waste

Prices keep rising in Sri Lanka. A school bag one child has outgrown can save another family a week of food money. A good shop down the road deserves more customers. LDF Market brings them together.

Shop plans help pay for the district teams who check items, and help keep LDF’s learning free.

Give what you can, find what you need
