SENDING MONEY

How to send from abroad

  1. Choose what to support in the app: a village batch, a programme day, or LDF in general.
  2. Send an international bank transfer in rupees or in your currency (LKR, USD, GBP, EUR or AUD), using the reference shown in the app.
  3. Tell us in the app so we can match it and thank you.
  4. Get the report with checked photos when the batch is delivered.

Card payments from abroad will come once LDF’s registration is complete.

Why it matters

  • Every rupee is shown openly in each batch’s money breakdown
  • Families are chosen fairly, never shown or named
  • Local shops pack, local teams deliver, so the money stays in the village
  • You get the completion report with photos
